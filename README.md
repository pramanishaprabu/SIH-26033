## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
A web-based platform called AgriConnect to help farmers find buyers and sell their agricultural products more easily.
Many farmers find it difficult to reach large markets on their own. They may also have to depend on middlemen to sell their products. Through AgriConnect, farmers can join together, list their available products and connect with retailers, supermarkets and bulk buyers.
When farmers sell their products as a group, they may be able to get better offers and reduce some of the extra costs involved in selling individually.

Key Features

1. Farmer Registration: Farmers can create an account and add their basic details.
2. Group Selling: Farmers growing similar products can join together and sell their products in larger quantities.
3. Buyer Connection: Retailers, supermarkets and wholesalers can find farmers and purchase products directly.
4. Bulk Order Management: Buyers can place large orders, and farmers can manage the quantity they can supply.
5. Market Price Updates: Farmers can check market prices before deciding how much to sell their products for.
6. AI-Based Buyer Matching: The system can suggest suitable buyers based on the products, quantity and location provided by farmers.
7. Transportation Support: Farmers can coordinate with other farmers to arrange transportation and share delivery costs.
8. Order Tracking: Farmers and buyers can check their order details and delivery status.
9. Regional Language Support: The platform can provide local language options so farmers can use it easily.

How This Solution Helps

AgriConnect gives farmers an opportunity to work together instead of selling everything individually. It helps them reach more buyers, manage bulk orders and reduce their dependence on multiple middlemen.
Our main aim is to make agricultural selling easier, improve farmers' earning opportunities and create a better connection between farmers and users

## Technical Approach
Develop AgriConnect as a web-based application. The website will allow farmers to register, join groups, list their products and connect with buyers.
We will use web development technologies along with Python and basic AI/ML features to build the platform.

Technologies Used

Frontend:HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Backend:Python (Flask)
Database:MySQL
AI/ML:Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn
Maps and location:OpenStreetMap

Implementation Steps

Step 1 – User Registration
Farmers and buyers can create their accounts and log in to the website.
Step 2 – Farmer Group Creation
Farmers growing similar products can join an existing group or create a new group to sell their products together.
Step 3 – Product Listing
Farmers can upload product names, images, available quantities, expected prices and location details.
Step 4 – Buyer Search
Retailers, supermarkets and wholesalers can search for products and find groups that can supply the required quantity.
Step 5 – AI-Based Buyer Matching
The system will analyse product type, quantity and location to suggest suitable buyers for each farmer group.
Step 6 – Bulk Order Management
Buyers can place bulk orders, and farmers can check the required quantity and confirm their availability.
Step 7 – Transportation Management
Farmers can coordinate transportation with other members of their group and plan deliveries together.
Step 8 – Order Tracking
Farmers and buyers can view order details and check the delivery status.

## Research and References
Research

We looked at some existing agricultural platforms and farmer organisations to understand how farmers can get better market opportunities.
1. National Agriculture Market (e-NAM)
e-NAM is an online platform that connects agricultural markets across India. It helps farmers and buyers access market information, participate in online trading and get better price information. We referred to this platform to understand how digital technology can improve agricultural marketing.
2.Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
FPOs help farmers come together and work as a group. They support farmers in marketing their products, improving their bargaining power and finding market opportunities. This gave us the idea of including group selling in AgriConnect. 
3. Digital Agricultural Marketing
Online agricultural platforms can connect farmers with buyers from different locations. They can also provide price information and make the buying and selling process more transparent. 
4. AI in Agriculture
AI and machine learning can be used to study product details, market information and buyer requirements. In our project, we plan to use these technologies to suggest suitable buyers based on product type, quantity and location.
